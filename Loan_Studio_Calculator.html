07 / Understand the result

How this loan estimate works

A few details help you read the figures without surprises.

EMI is not all principal

Each payment first covers the month’s interest on the outstanding balance. The remainder reduces principal. As the balance falls, the interest portion normally shrinks and the principal portion grows.

Extra payments can cut interest

In this model, extra repayments reduce principal after the selected interval’s EMI. They keep the regular EMI unchanged and generally shorten the loan. Check your lender’s prepayment rules and any charges.

Upfront costs are separate

Processing fees and other costs are added to estimated cash outflow, but not to the principal amortization. Actual taxes, insurance terms and lender charges depend on your loan offer.

Estimates, not a lender quote

The calculation assumes a constant annual rate, monthly payments and a reducing balance. Floating rates, payment-date conventions, rounding and lender-specific methods can change the final schedule.

Built for planning, not predicting lender statements. Review your sanction letter for the final terms.
